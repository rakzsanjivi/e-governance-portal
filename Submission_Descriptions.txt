Week 1 Project Planning and Requirements Analysis

This report presents the week 1 project planning and requirements analysis for CivicServe, a proposed e-governance and digital services portal. It addresses project scope, user personas, functional requirements, milestones, and risks in a self-contained format intended for internship evaluation and later implementation review. The recommended portal allows residents to discover illustrative public services, prepare requests, receive references, and follow decisions. Department staff have a separate review journey with recorded reasons and controlled access to evidence.

The report explains the decisions behind the proposed approach and gives practical acceptance conditions rather than treating general recommendations as completed features. It distinguishes the four-week planning deliverables from later engineering and public deployment work. The initial scope uses residence certificate requests, income certificate requests, and civic grievances as examples. Real certificate issuance, live government identity verification, payments, and external document integrations require separate approvals and are outside the submitted package.

Supporting artifacts make the document actionable. The repository contains responsive static wireframes, Mermaid journey and security diagrams, a PostgreSQL starter schema, an OpenAPI contract, a risk register, and a review checklist. These files share the same service and application concepts across all four weeks. Public references are identified where they support accessibility, government website governance, framework security, or compliance review. Proposed performance and recovery figures are clearly labelled as targets that require later measurement.

The submission is a completed planning project, not a claim that a production government service has been implemented or certified. Before a pilot, department owners should validate content, user needs, current legal requirements, retention rules, security controls, and operational responsibilities. This report provides the relevant design rationale and review steps needed to move from an internship planning exercise to a controlled implementation phase.
